FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Reach out any time — we're happy to help you find your footing.

Is BridgeDDS really free?

Yes, completely. BridgeDDS is a free national mentorship network. There are no fees for pre-dental students or for mentors — ever.

Who are the mentors?

Current dental students from schools across the country who volunteer their time to guide pre-dental students through the application process.

How does matching work?

You tell us about your goals, background, and target states, and we pair you with a mentor whose experience fits what you're looking for.

How much time does mentorship take?

It's flexible. Most pairs meet for as little as one hour a month, on a schedule that works for both of you.

I am not sure dental school is right for me. Can I still join?

Absolutely. A mentor can help you figure out whether dentistry is the right path before you commit to the application process.

Can I become a mentor while in dental school?

Yes. Current dental students are exactly who we're looking for — your experience is what makes the mentorship valuable.
