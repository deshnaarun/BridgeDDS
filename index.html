How It Works

Mentorship made simple.

Three thoughtful steps stand between you and a mentor who has been exactly where you are.

01

Apply

Tell us about your goals, your target schools, and where you are in your journey. It takes just a few minutes.

02

Get Matched

We pair you with a current dental student who fits your interests, background, and the states you care about.

03

Grow

Meet regularly, get honest guidance on the DAT, applications, and shadowing, and build lasting momentum.
